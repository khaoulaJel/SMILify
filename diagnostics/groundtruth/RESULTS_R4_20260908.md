12 × 12 can hide a bimodal effect — and V10 and V13 both showed this project's medians can be

## Reading

The effect is **not** a subset artefact — it holds on every landmark that is not on the head, and on
every specimen but one. The spread across specimens (5.4% to 63.4%) tracks how unusual the animal
is: *Eciton*, with long crossed sabre mandibles, is worst; *Leptogenys*, a generic-proportioned
ponerine, is best. **The named parts drift furthest on exactly the morphologies a morphometric study
most wants to measure.**

## Limits

- n = 12, one run per arm. Reachability, not generalisation — all landmarks are supervised.
- Part membership is by dominant skinning weight; `an_3_r`/`an_3_l` carry only 4 vertices each, so
  distal antennal segments are barely represented in the template at all.
- The correct-part assignment is anatomical judgement, not derived. A different reasonable
  assignment would move individual rows, not the 0.2%-versus-34.7% separation.
