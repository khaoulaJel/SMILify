# V9 — the two anatomical channels are ORTHOGONAL. Skeletal supervision does not reach the skin,
# and the combined objective buys both gains for the price of one.

Job 3838890, 3m52s, 12 annotated specimens. All four arms judged on the **same four
never-supervised surface landmarks** (`mandibular_apex_l`, `antennal_insertion_l`, `scape_apex_l`,
`wl_posterior_r`). In arm C those landmarks enter no objective at all — the evaluation quantity is
a different *kind* of observation from the supervision, so it cannot be memorised.

**VOIDING CONTROL PASSES.** Arm A (re-optimised, no supervision) holds at production: held-out
13.4% vs 12.9%, chamfer 1.02×.

| arm | **HELD-OUT surface** | sup surface | **joint err** | chamfer | ×prod | viol | asym | deform |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| production | 12.9% | 8.7% | 33.22% | 0.00081 | 1.00× | 1 | 0.074 | 3.91 |
| **A** D1 | 13.4% | 8.2% | 32.80% | 0.00083 | 1.02× | 1 | 0.044 | 5.68 |
| **B** +landmarks | **7.6%** | 0.2% | 32.94% | 0.00087 | **1.07×** | 1 | 0.101 | 5.80 |
| **C** +joints | 12.9% | 8.7% | **15.91%** | 0.00130 | 1.60× | 0 | 0.052 | 7.72 |
| **D** +both | **7.1%** | 0.1% | **15.33%** | 0.00129 | 1.59× | 1 | 0.040 | 7.83 |

## The missing arrow: joints → surface is ABSENT

**C moves held-out surface error 13.4% → 12.9% (+4%)** while moving joint error 32.8% → 15.9%.
A 51% correction to the skeleton produces a **4%** change in surface anatomy — well inside the
noise of n=12 with one run per arm.

Combined with V8's finding that surface → joints is also absent (33.2% → 33.0%), **the two channels
are independent in both directions.** Correcting where the rotation pivots sit does not move the
skin toward the anatomy a human identifies on it, and vice versa.

This is the "C does nothing but D ≈ B" outcome. It is **not** the jackpot scenario — there is no
synergy — but it is a clean structural result about the model.

## Complementarity: additive, not synergistic — and that is enough

**D ≈ B on the surface** (7.1% vs 7.6%, a 6% difference, i.e. noise) and **D ≈ C on the joints**
(15.33% vs 15.91%). The combined objective gets **each channel's full benefit and no interaction
term**.

The practical consequence is better than synergy would suggest at first glance:

> **D delivers both anatomical improvements — surface 13.4% → 7.1% (−47%) and joints 32.8% → 15.3%
> (−53%) — at 1.59× chamfer, which is the price of the joint term ALONE (C is 1.60×).**

The landmark term is essentially free once the joint term is being paid for. **D dominates C**: same
surface cost, same joint accuracy, and half the surface-anatomy error.

## The compensation hypothesis is NOT supported

`deform_verts` RMS: production 3.91 → A 5.68 → **C 7.72** → **D 7.83**. The joint term inflates
deformation, reproducing V1's 2.4× finding — and **adding landmark supervision does not suppress
it** (D is marginally *higher* than C). The idea that surface anatomy would constrain the
deformation field and stop it absorbing skeletal corrections is not borne out.

## What this licenses

**A candidate production change**: production objective + joint-position term (λ≈0.03) +
point-to-surface landmark term (λ≈1). Both anatomical endpoints roughly halve; chamfer costs 1.59×;
trait violations stay flat at 0–1.

It is **not deployable as-is** — both terms need human annotations at inference, which do not exist
outside these 12 specimens. What V9 establishes is *what the objective is missing and what supplying
it is worth*, not a shippable method. The deployment path (a predictor supplying the targets) still
carries G8's ~75%-coverage requirement and G9's structured-noise caveat, neither of which V9 touches.

## Limits

- **n = 12, one run per arm, no seed replication.** This fitter shows ~0.01 run-to-run variation
  (Z4/Z5). **The +4% (C vs A) and +6% (D vs B) differences are not readable** and are reported as
  null, not as small effects.
- **Arm B's bilateral asymmetry rises to 0.101** from A's 0.044. The held-out split supervises the
  right-side landmarks and holds out the left-side ones, so this is plausibly an artifact of the
  split rather than of landmark supervision — V10 (rotating the split) is the test, and D's 0.040
  argues against it being a real effect.
- Joint error is still measured on the joints used as targets. G8's no-propagation result is
  untouched.
- Held-out surface error at 7.1% is below V7's 10.1% nearest-surface floor and approaching
  **unmeasured human placement variability**. Until one specimen is annotated twice, it is not known
  whether 7.1% is real headroom or the instrument's ceiling.
