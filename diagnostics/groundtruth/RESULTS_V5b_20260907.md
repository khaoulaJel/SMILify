## The finding

**V5's central claim is WITHDRAWN.** V5 reported that violations climb monotonically (1 → 2 → 5 →
6 → 8) as joint error collapses, and concluded that anatomical accuracy and surface-derived
biological validity are *in conflict*. That trend **does not exist**. On the corrected ruler the
violation column is flat at 0–1 across the entire sweep, with no relationship to λ.

The climb was the mandible landmark. Supervision moves the fit; a ruler attached 12.6% of a body
length from the mandible tip reads that movement as a growing violation. Correct the ruler and the
signal disappears.

## What replaces it

**Joint supervision improves anatomy at a real but bounded surface cost, and costs trait validity
nothing.** At λ=0.03: joint error **32.74% → 15.20%** (−54%) against expert annotations, with
**zero** biologically impossible traits, for **1.54×** chamfer.

**A trade-off still exists — it is just a different one.** Anatomy costs *chamfer*, roughly
proportionally, and that has not changed. What is refuted is that it costs *biological validity*.
Those were being conflated because the same broken landmarks generated both numbers.

## Consequences

1. **The landmark arm is no longer a rescue mission.** V8 was motivated by "joint supervision
   damages the surface morphology, so supply anatomy on the surface instead". That premise is gone.
   V8's question becomes sharper and smaller: can surface observations buy the same anatomical gain
   for **less chamfer** than 1.54×?
2. **V4's hinge baseline is moot.** It removed violations that, on the corrected ruler, largely do
   not exist — production shows 1 violation across these 12 specimens, and 6.5% corpus-wide rather
   than 8.7%.
3. **Joint supervision is now a candidate shippable change on its own**, which it was not while it
   appeared to destroy trait validity.

## Limits

- Violations here are counted over **12 specimens**, so the counts are 0–6 and the power to detect
  a trend is low. The flat column is consistent with "no effect" but does not prove a small one
  absent. The corpus-scale version of this test is the one that would settle it.
- Joint error is still measured on the **same joints used as targets** — reachability, not
  generalisation. G8 showed the benefit does not propagate to unsupervised joints below ~75%
  coverage, and that limit is untouched here.
- Chamfer 1.54× at the operating point is a real cost that nothing in this run justifies as
  acceptable; that judgement needs the downstream use.
