# V3 — the anterior is NOT a pose/shape degeneracy. The violations are a mild, continuous,
# *predictable* drift — which is a much easier problem.

Run 2026-09-07 (login node, 4 threads, niced — the cluster queue was estimating 6 h to 3 days and
this is a short read-only analysis). Pre-registered in `PREREGISTRATION_V3_identifiability.md`.
**No ground truth used anywhere.** 69 flagged + 69 matched unflagged specimens.

**NUMERICAL CHECK PASSES EXACTLY.** The smallest principal angle shifts **0.00°** between step
sizes 1e-2 and 1e-3, so the finite differencing is converged and the subspace geometry is real.

## H1 FAILS — the anterior is not degenerate

Smallest principal angle between the anterior POSE and SHAPE subspaces, at each specimen's own
production parameters:

| region | median | p10 | p90 | # angles < 10° (median) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| anterior | **12.14°** | 10.05 | 15.61 | **0** |
| gaster (control) | **18.40°** | 15.98 | 21.26 | **0** |

The anterior *is* more entangled than the gaster, in the predicted direction — but by 6.3°, not the
10° the bar required, and **not a single specimen in either region has any principal angle below
10°**. Pose and shape are separated everywhere. A surface objective *can* tell them apart.

## H2 FAILS decisively — and this is what kills the hypothesis

| | median smallest angle |
|---|---:|
| flagged (pathological) | 12.10° |
| unflagged | 12.14° |

One-sided Mann–Whitney **p = 0.476**. **The degeneracy is identical in fits that go wrong and fits
that don't.** Whatever separates the 69 from the 688, it is not subspace geometry. Even had H1
passed on its margin, H2 removes any causal reading.

**Corroborating, independently:** changing shape moves the anterior joints only **0.104×** as much
as changing pose does (0.00616 vs 0.05944 per unit). β is *not* acting as a pose surrogate. The
"β compensates for bad head pose" mechanism is refuted from a second direction.

## H3 PASSES — the violations are predictable from the parameters

L2 logistic regression on 25 βs + 27 anterior pose parameters, 5-fold cross-validated:
**AUC 0.760** against a label-permutation null of **0.493** (bar was 0.75).

Which fits will emit biologically impossible traits is **substantially determined by the fitted
parameters themselves**. That is a QC filter available on the whole corpus today, with no
annotation, no ground truth and no refitting.

## The finding that reframes the problem: severity

Max fractional overshoot past the bound, over the 69 flagged:

| p10 | p25 | p50 | p75 | p90 | max |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.018 | 0.063 | **0.178** | 0.356 | 0.717 | **1.958** |

**68% of flagged specimens are within 25% of the bound. Not one exceeds 2× the bound width.**

This is a **continuous drift, not a bimodal catastrophe.** There is no population of fits that
switched into an alternate parameterisation — the distribution is smooth and concentrated just past
the line. Which is exactly the regime where **a weak plausibility regulariser is sufficient**, and
exactly the regime where mode-switching, multi-start, basin-hopping and continuation are *not*
needed. The severity diagnostic was proposed to distinguish those two worlds; it came back
unambiguously on the easier side.

## What this means, and what to do

Three mechanisms are now dead: deformation (V2), pose/shape identifiability (V3 H1/H2), and β
acting as a pose compensator (the 0.104 ratio). What replaces them is more tractable, not less:

> **The impossible traits are a mild, continuous, parameter-predictable drift just past the
> biological bounds — not a structural failure of the model or the objective.**

**The indicated fix is the soft plausibility constraint** — a hinge penalty active only outside the
bounds, zero inside them, so it cannot distort real biology:

    L_trait = max(0, r_min - r)^2 + max(0, r - r_max)^2

Before V3 this was a guess. Now it is matched to a measured severity distribution: median overshoot
0.178 of the bound width means a *weak* penalty is enough, and the absence of any catastrophic tail
means nothing needs rescuing from a distant basin. And H3 says the affected fits can be identified
in advance, so the penalty can be applied selectively or used purely as QC.

## Limits

- Principal angles are **local**, first-order at the fitted parameters. They bound what gradient
  descent can distinguish *nearby*, not the global landscape.
- The anterior-vs-gaster gap (12.14° vs 18.40°) is real and in the predicted direction. It is
  reported because it is true, not because it supports the hypothesis — H2 shows it does not
  separate pathological from healthy fits, so it explains nothing about the 69.
- AUC 0.760 is a corpus-internal predictor of a *bound-based flag*, not of anatomical correctness.
- Trait validity, never accuracy. Still no surface ground truth.
- Run on the login node rather than via sbatch, contrary to standing practice; justified by the
  queue (6 h–3 day estimates) and the job's size (4 threads, niced, no GPU, minutes).
