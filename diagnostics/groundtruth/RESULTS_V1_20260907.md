**VOIDING CONTROL PASSES.** `L0_free` re-optimised from production stays at production
(chamfer 1.03×, joints 36.9% vs 38.9%), so every row measures λ and the deform mode, not the
optimiser.

## H1 FAILS — and the failure names its own cause

Biologically impossible traits go **1 → 6** at λ=0.1 and ML bilateral asymmetry doubles
(0.088 → 0.175). The joint term as G7 ran it (deform free) **does** damage the biological surface
representation. G7's headline was measured on an endpoint that could not see this.

But the damage is not caused by the joint term. Across all nine arms, impossible-trait count
tracks `deform_verts` magnitude almost perfectly — **Spearman 0.932, Pearson 0.945** — and the
violations are exactly the traits whose landmarks sit on thin, freely-deformable structures
(HW/HL ×4, ML/HL, SL/HL). Zero deformation gives **zero** violations in both arms that have it,
with or without the joint term.

## H2 FAILS on the deform half; the other half was vacuous

`deform_verts` RMS grows **0.702% → 1.687%** (2.4×) at λ=0.1. The joint term is partly satisfied by
per-vertex surface hacking, so its joint-error number overstates the anatomical gain.

**`log_beta_scales` is unchanged at 0.2317 in every single arm because it is not in the optimised
parameter set** (`FREE_ALL` covers global_rot, joint_rot, betas, trans, deform_verts). That half of
the bar tested nothing and is withdrawn rather than counted as a pass.

## H3 FAILS, with the sign reversed — deformation is NOT the escape route

The hypothesis was that free deformation absorbs the surface mismatch and is therefore why the
objective is blind to the skeleton (G6); freezing it should let the joint term reach further.
The opposite happens: freezing deform makes joints **worse**, 9.12% → 17.52%, and zeroing it gives
16.43%. And removing deformation on its own does nothing for anatomy (`L0_dzero` 36.65% ≈
production 38.86%).

**Deformation is not hiding the skeleton — it is helping the joint term reach it.** G6's
under-determination has some other source. This closes the "freeze deform during the anatomical
stage" idea as a fix.

## The result that actually matters: `L01_dzero`

| | production | `L01_dzero` |
|---|---:|---:|
| joint residual | 38.86% | **16.43%** (−58%) |
| biologically impossible traits | 1 | **0** |
| ML bilateral asymmetry | 0.088 | 0.117 (+33%) |
| chamfer | 0.00095 | 0.00503 (5.29×) |

**Joint term ON, deformation OFF cuts joint error by 58% while producing zero impossible traits.**
It is the only arm that improves anatomy without degrading trait validity.

The chamfer cost decomposes cleanly and roughly multiplicatively: disabling deform alone costs
2.26×, the joint term alone costs 2.31×, both together 5.29× ≈ 2.26 × 2.34. The two costs are
close to independent, so this is not a compounding pathology — it is two separately-priced changes.

## What this changes

1. **G7's 9.0% is not deliverable as measured.** It is bought partly with inflated deformation and
   it multiplies impossible traits sixfold. Report it with this caveat attached.
2. **T1's 9.1% impossible-trait rate now has a mechanism.** T1 proposed area-sampled inflation and
   T2 refuted it. V1 supplies a different candidate with a direct demonstration rather than a
   correlation: turn `deform_verts` off and the violations go to zero. This is testable on the full
   757-fit corpus without any annotation.
3. **Deformation is a trait-validity liability, not a skeleton-hiding mechanism.** Both of those
   are new, and the second is the refutation of the hypothesis this run was designed around.

## Limits

- **n = 14, one optimisation per arm, no seed replication.** Z4/Z5 measured ~0.01 run-to-run
  variation on this fitter; differences under ~10% relative are not readable here.
- **No surface ground truth.** Impossible-trait count and bilateral asymmetry are validity and
  self-consistency, not accuracy. A trait can be inside every bound and still wrong. The five
  expert surface landmarks (T4) remain what would settle it.
- **The 0.932 correlation is across 9 non-independent arms**, so it is descriptive. The
  load-bearing evidence is the direct contrast: deform zero → 0 violations, in both arms.
- **Chamfer 5.29× is a real cost** and is not yet weighed against anything. Whether it matters
  depends on what the surface is for, which V1 does not measure.
