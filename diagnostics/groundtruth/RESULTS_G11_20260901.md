## Three things this settles

**1. Shrinkage is refuted.** My hypothesis was that the fit compresses between-specimen variation.
It does the opposite: spread is **1.38**, i.e. *inflated* relative to ground truth, as is production
(1.32) and the noisy targets most of all (1.74, since noise adds variance). **G9 and G10 are
therefore still unreconciled** — the fit reduces absolute joint error while achieving lower trait
correlation, and over-dispersion rather than shrinkage is what needs explaining.

**2. Relaxing the shape prior does nothing on its own.** Without the joint term, every clamp from
|z| ≤ 1 to free gives R ≈ −0.06, indistinguishable from production. The prior is **not** what is
suppressing trait reliability, and "loosen the prior" is not a fix.

**3. The joint term robustly rescues the traits.** R goes from ≈ −0.06 to ≈ **+0.31** and stays
there across all four clamp levels (0.305–0.316) — a large, stable effect that does not depend on
the clamp at all. Joint error falls 38.7% → 19% at 2.5× chamfer. **This replicates G10's
qualitative finding on an independent noise draw**, which is the part of G10 that survives.

## What it does NOT settle, and the auto-verdict was wrong

The script concluded "the relaxed prior lifts the fit above reading the skeleton directly, G10's
PARTIAL becomes a PASS". **That is not supported, on two counts.**

**The clamp is irrelevant.** 0.306 at |z| ≤ 1 against 0.316 at |z| ≤ 3 is noise, not a treatment
effect. Nothing here recommends relaxing the prior.

**J-vs-T flips with the random seed.** G10 used seed 200, G11 seed 97, same σ:

| | G10 | G11 |
|---|---:|---:|
| T | +0.336 | +0.262 |
| J | +0.171 | +0.306 |
| **J − T** | **−0.164** | **+0.044** |

The seed-to-seed swing (~0.14) is as large as the gap being measured. **A single noise realisation
cannot separate J from T at n = 14**, so G10's PARTIAL and G11's PASS are equally unreliable on that
specific comparison. Everything else in both runs stands, because it does not rest on one draw.

## The one honest conclusion available now

**Adding a joint-position term takes trait reliability from no signal (R ≈ −0.06) to real signal
(R ≈ +0.31), robustly and independently of the shape prior.** Whether the fitted skeleton then beats
simply reading the predicted skeleton is **unresolved** and requires multiple noise draws — which is
G12, submitted.
