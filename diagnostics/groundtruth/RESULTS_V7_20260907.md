## Two findings, and the second is the important one

**1. The recalibration generalises.** 53.6% → 44.2% on specimens that contributed nothing to the
calibration, 8 of 10 landmarks improving. V6's correction is real, not memorised.

**2. It is nowhere near sufficient, and the reason is structural.** LOO sits at **4.4× the floor**:
**77% of the remaining error is the landmark index, not the fit.** The fitted surface passes within
**10.1%** of Weber's length of the human landmark; the best fixed vertex we can choose is **44.2%**
away.

**A single template vertex cannot represent an anatomical landmark across these species.** The 12
specimens' independent votes disagree by 6–17% of a body length (V6), so no one index satisfies
them — the correspondence between template vertex and anatomy is not stable enough to carry a
named landmark.

## What this means for the trait layer

The whole GLAD layer is built on "one template vertex = one anatomical point". At this
correspondence quality that design has a **floor it cannot go below**, and the floor is four times
worse than the fit it is trying to measure. **Every trait number in T1, T3, T4 and V4 inherits it.**

It also reframes the project's central puzzle. G13 found joint-derived traits score R ≈ 0 against
expert truth and T4 found surface traits carry 2.8× more genus signal — but both were measured
*through the same unstable correspondence*. **The measurement instrument, not the fitter, may be
what has been limiting the morphometrics all along.** That is consistent with the entire W-series,
which found within-part correspondence to be the weak link and never closed it.

## The concrete consequence for the 2x2

A landmark supervision term must **not** be written as "template vertex v should sit at human point
p" — that asserts a correspondence the data says is unstable, and would train the fit to satisfy a
wrong identity. It should be **point-to-surface**: the fitted surface should pass through p, with no
claim about which vertex does it.

That is a real design decision the pilot data has just made for us, and it would not have been
visible without the held-out split.

## Limits

- n = 12, and LOO at n = 12 is high-variance; the two landmarks that got *worse*
  (`antennal_insertion_l`, `scape_apex_r`) may be that rather than a real regression.
- The votes conflate correspondence error, human placement variability and genuine interspecific
  anatomy. V7 bounds their total, not each separately.
- The floor is computed on the production fit; a better fit would lower it and raise the share
  attributable to correspondence further.
