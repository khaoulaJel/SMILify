# Ground-truth contamination audit — what may be presented, what must not

**2026-09-16.** Triggered by the rule: *only results validated against the corrected expert
annotations may be presented, and they must not be compared against pre-correction numbers.*

Every claim below was checked against files on disk, not against memory. Two probes in this folder
produce the numbers: `frame_contamination_PROBE.py`, `recal_sanity_PROBE.py`.

---

## Summary

The frame bug is worse than "a correction to some numbers". **The experiments that produced the
Arc-2 storyline (R3 → R9 → A1 → A3, and the 2026-09-16 slide8 rebuild) supervised the model onto
landmarks displaced a median of 50.3% of Weber's length from the true anatomy — twice the
production fit's own joint error of 25.1% WL.** Their "anatomically correct" arm was not correct.
Every conclusion drawn from the comparison is void, and the direction of the result is explained by
the bug rather than by the objective.

The clean replacement already exists and is stronger: **the Joint-Alignment Benchmark**
(`diagnostics/joint_alignment_benchmark/`), pre-registered with a frozen SHA-256, n = 11 expert
specimens, correct frame, 396 fits.

## 3. What replaces the dead storyline

JAB already contains a *better*, validated version of the same scientific point:

- **Production skeleton error: 25.1% WL** (95% CI 17.5–44.8) — and fixing the ruler alone moved
  this from 42.7% → 25.1%, i.e. the first finding is about the measurement, not the fitter.
- **Surface and skeleton dissociate (arm J).** Removing the offset/normal penalties gives the
  *best surface of any strategy* (0.45× chamfer) and a *worse* skeleton (×1.25 joint-level,
  worse on 8/11). This is "the score can lie", measured correctly — and it is the **opposite
  sign** to what A1 claimed.
- **No single-factor change improves joint alignment**; D1_PROD has the best mean rank of 12
  (Friedman p = 0.011).
- **Expert supervision shows the reachable ceiling but does not transfer**: all-joint supervision
  26.9 → 17.4% WL (10/11), yet holding one region out leaves that region unchanged (every CI
  includes 0). Anatomical constraints act locally.
- **Region verdict, directly presentable**: coxae 11.6, trochanter/femur 12.9, body axis 24.1,
  mandibles 42.8, antennae 54.9, tibia/tarsus 56.3% WL. Proximal joints are usable; distal legs,
  antennae and mandibles are not.
- **The error is specimen- and joint-driven**, not strategy-driven (joint variance 0.36, specimen
  0.17, optimiser seed 0.02 on the log scale).

## 4. Recommended handling (nothing deleted yet)

Per the project's own rule that diagnostic artifacts stay on disk until reviewed, **nothing has
been moved or deleted.** Proposed, pending approval:

1. Quarantine — `git mv` the void scripts and their result folders under
   `diagnostics/VOID_frame_contaminated_20260916/`, each keeping its RESULTS file, with this audit
   linked. Reversible; preserves the record of what was tried.
2. Prepend a `> **VOID — see AUDIT_20260916.md**` banner to every affected RESULTS/`.md` file so no
   future reader (or model) re-quotes the numbers.
3. Re-derive, in order: (a) the landmark recalibration from frame-corrected coordinates, which
   unblocks (b) M4's scale-free traits and (c) the Atta physical agreement.
4. Leave JAB untouched. It is the acceptance gate and the presentation's evidential spine.
