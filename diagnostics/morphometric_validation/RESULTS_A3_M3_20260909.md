## A3 — verdict FAIL, via the pre-registered "guard broken" row

| | arm A (D1_PROD) | arm B (w_offset=0, w_normal=0) | |
|---|--:|--:|---|
| **median trait error** | **8.53%** | **17.54%** | **+105.7%**, Wilcoxon p=3.18e-12, B better on **3/48** |
| **correspondence guard** | 0.0625 | 0.0817 | **+30.8%** against a ≤+5% bar → **BROKEN** |
| deform magnitude | 0.0027 | 0.0435 | **+1515%** |

Per trait, arm B is worse on **all nine**: SL +576%, FL +114%, HL +136%, HW +103%, TBL +55%,
PetL +46%, GL +42%, WL +31%, ML +22%.

**Mechanism, visible in one row.** Without the `offset` penalty, `deform_verts` explodes **16x**.
Free-form deformation then absorbs the fit, destroying vertex correspondence (+30.8%) and the
morphometrics with it. This is exactly Z4/Z5's finding (deform is per-specimen noise; `offset` is
what bounds it), and exactly the outcome this preregistration named as most likely before running.

**Why A1 said the opposite, and why both are correct.** A1's CORRECT arm was landmark-supervised in
every configuration — the landmarks anchored the solution while `offset` was removed. A1 therefore
measured whether the objective *fights* the right answer (it does, and that reverses). A3 measures
whether an *unsupervised* fit *finds* it (it does not, catastrophically). **The objective's
preference was never the binding constraint on unsupervised recovery.**

> **Consequence, fixed in advance: the objective-design branch closes.** No further loss-weight
> campaign. The bottleneck is identity/search.

`offset` is load-bearing and must stay. A1's result stands as a statement about the objective's
preference landscape, not as a recipe change — which is what its own limits section already said.

## M3 — reproducibility across 3 seeds (seed 0 = A3 arm A, seeds 1-2 fresh)

Same config, same 48 specimens, different seeds. CV is per-specimen across seeds; recovery error is
the 3-seed mean vs exact ground truth.

**Three findings:**

1. **The secondary slot is eliminated, not filled.** FL was the only candidate and fails the
   reproducibility gate (4.14% mean, **40.9% max**) on top of its rig-joint definitional problem.
   The panel finalises at **HW + HL primary, no secondary**.
2. **PetL is unstable, not merely unvalidatable** — up to **93.1%** variation on the same specimen
   between seeds. Its exclusion is now over-determined.
3. **Error is mostly systematic, not stochastic.** CV/err is 0.11-0.25 for most traits, so optimizer
   noise is a minority of total error — the recovery gap is real, not sampling. The exceptions are
   SL (0.45) and FL (0.42), where nearly half the error is seed noise alone.

## Limits

48 synthetic specimens, in-model, noise-free, shared topology. Three seeds is a small sample for a
max-CV statistic — the max values (93%, 44%, 41%) identify unstable traits reliably but their
magnitudes are not precise. A3's arms differ only in the four weight lines (diff-verified).
