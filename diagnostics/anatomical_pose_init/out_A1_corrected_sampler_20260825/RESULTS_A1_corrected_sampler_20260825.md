# A1 — Corrected Pose Sampler: Results (2026-08-25, completed 2026-08-26)

**Bottom line:** the corrected sampler is real and verified (AR(1) chain coupling 0.05→0.52,
magnitude unchanged) but does not, by itself, close the representational gap the rest of this
investigation is built around. Both A1 checks that could show an interaction — the disentangling
probe (coxa-only-vs-overall gap) and now the basin-map rerun (12/12 conditions, sign+Wilcoxon,
outlier-excluded deltas) — came back null, pre-registered before either was run. This is a clean
result: the sampler fixes corpus *realism*, the architecture work (A3, Phase 10) fixes
*representation* — two genuinely separate problems, confirmed rather than assumed to be separate.

## What was built

`generate_correlated_chain_parameters` (`fitter_3d/pointcloud2smil/sample_smil_model.py`),
additive alongside the unmodified `generate_random_parameters`. Each leg's rotating chain
(co→tr→fe→ti→ta) is drawn as a lag-1 AR(1) process (`rot_i = rho*rot_{i-1} +
sqrt(1-rho^2)*eps_i`) instead of fully independently; non-leg joints stay i.i.d. `rho=0` is
verified byte-identical to `generate_random_parameters` given the same seed (confirmed, not
assumed). `make_synth_corpus.py` gained `--sampler {iid,correlated} --rho` flags, default
`iid` (legacy behavior unchanged when unspecified).

## Calibration caveat — stated up front, not buried

**There is no independent real-pose corpus in this project to fit `rho` against.**
`synth_clean` is itself generated by the i.i.d. sampler this function replaces (via
`make_synth_corpus.py` → `generate_random_parameters`), so an initial attempt to calibrate `rho`
from `synth_clean`'s own lag-1 axis-direction correlation was circular — it measured the old
sampler's own artifact (pooled lag-1 cosine +0.035, i.e. ≈0, exactly as an i.i.d. sampler
would produce) and would have "confirmed" rho≈0, defeating the point. This extends finding #8
(no corpus supports a learned pose prior) to this narrower question: there's no data to fit even
a single coupling scalar against, not just insufficient data for a high-dimensional subspace.
**`rho` is therefore a swept design choice, motivated by the basin-map coherent-vs-incoherent gap
(#3), not a fitted parameter** — reported here as such.

## rho sweep — verification the intervention does what it claims

Three 12-specimen corpora generated (`diagnostics/moonshot/anatomical_pose_init_corr_rho{00,03,06}`),
same seed/pose_scale/shape_scale as `synth_clean`, differing only in sampler/rho:

| rho | pooled lag-1 direction cosine (co-tr / tr-fe / fe-ti / ti-ta) | pooled per-joint magnitude mean |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0 (= old i.i.d.) | +0.083 / +0.118 / +0.061 / -0.062 (pooled +0.050) | 22.16° |
| 0.3 | +0.225 / +0.248 / +0.228 / +0.149 (pooled +0.212) | 21.88° |
| 0.6 | +0.509 / +0.550 / +0.521 / +0.489 (pooled +0.517) | 21.83° |

Coupling rises monotonically and roughly tracks the target rho; per-joint magnitude stays flat
(~22°, std ~9°) across all three — **confirms the intervention changes chain coupling only, not
magnitude scale**, by construction (AR(1) preserves marginal variance) and now verified
empirically. Deliverable (i) plot: `fig_A1_pose_magnitude_old_vs_new_sampler.png` (old vs rho=0.6
histograms, near-total overlap as expected).

`rho=0.6` was carried forward as "the corrected sampler" for the rest of A1 (clearest,
most-separated signal from the sweep; `rho=0.3` corpus is kept on disk for reference/reruns if the
0.6 result needs a weaker-coupling check).

## Disentangling probe rerun — a null result, reported plainly

`coherent_multi_estimator_oracle_PROBE.py` rerun on `synth_clean` (old) vs
`anatomical_pose_init_corr_rho06` (corrected), N=12 specimens x 6 legs = 72 chains each:

| | old (i.i.d.) | corrected (rho=0.6) |
|---|---:|---:|
| coxa-only-vs-overall-direction gap, mean | 50.03° | 50.46° |
| median | 49.82° | 46.52° |
| p95 | 99.33° | 97.99° |

**Essentially unchanged.** Strong lag-1 axis coupling between chain-adjacent joints (pooled
cosine 0.05→0.52) does not shrink the gap that makes coxa-only estimators structurally lossy.
This is consistent with the gap being a nonlinear consequence of composing five independent-
magnitude rotations through forward kinematics — correlating the *axis directions* of adjacent
joints doesn't stop their composition from producing a net rotation a single coxa-only rotation
can't reproduce, at this coupling strength. Reported as a genuine null result (per this
investigation's own discipline, #12/closing notes): the corrected sampler does not, by itself,
change the previously-established structural case for correspondence-based approaches (§4-5 of
`SESSION_SYNTHESIS_20260825.md`).

## Deliverable (ii) — basin-map leg_acc curves, old vs corrected sampler: RESULT

All 13 conditions fit+eval'd (`run_basin_map_corr_rho06_20260825.sh`, 2026-08-26). Pre-registered
in `PREREGISTRATION_basin_map_leg_acc_20260825.md` before running: Prediction 1 (orthogonality —
ordering and gap sizes preserved, most deltas within ±0.03–0.05) vs Prediction 2 (a genuine
interaction between corpus coupling and perturbation recoverability).

**Ordering preserved exactly at every magnitude**: `proximal` worst, `coherent`/`distal` best,
`random` in between, matching the original basin map's qualitative story — including the
headline result itself (coherent vs proximal at 30°, matched magnitude: gap **widened** slightly
under the corrected sampler, 0.252 vs the original 0.217).

**Aggregate deltas, then the statistical check the pre-registration committed to** (sign test +
Wilcoxon across all 12 specimens, plus outlier-excluded mean, per this investigation's standing
discipline):

| condition | mag | old | corrected | Δ (aggregate) | sign p | Wilcoxon p | Δ (outlier-excluded) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| random | 15° | 0.929 | 0.949 | +0.020 | 1.000 | 0.970 | −0.005 |
| random | 23° | 0.875 | 0.893 | +0.019 | 1.000 | 1.000 | −0.016 |
| random | 30° | 0.817 | 0.808 | −0.008 | 1.000 | 0.910 | −0.013 |
| proximal | 15° | 0.863 | 0.917 | +0.054 | 0.774 | 1.000 | −0.004 |
| proximal | 23° | 0.775 | 0.751 | −0.024 | 0.774 | 0.970 | +0.057 |
| proximal | 30° | 0.622 | 0.664 | +0.042 | 0.774 | 0.970 | +0.022 |
| distal | 15° | 0.937 | 0.949 | +0.012 | 0.388 | 0.910 | −0.008 |
| distal | 23° | 0.938 | 0.948 | +0.010 | 1.000 | 0.791 | −0.010 |
| distal | 30° | 0.940 | 0.949 | +0.010 | 0.774 | 0.677 | −0.012 |
| coherent | 15° | 0.937 | 0.950 | +0.013 | 1.000 | 0.850 | −0.009 |
| coherent | 23° | 0.937 | 0.929 | −0.008 | 0.774 | 0.910 | +0.004 |
| coherent | 30° | 0.839 | 0.916 | **+0.077** | 0.388 | 0.380 | **−0.002** |

**None of the 12 conditions reach significance** (sign p ≥ 0.388, Wilcoxon p ≥ 0.380 throughout).
`coherent_30deg`'s aggregate delta looked like the one exception (+0.077, the largest in the
table) — but its outlier-excluded mean is −0.002 and its specimen split (8/4) doesn't clear even
a loose significance bar. Same pattern the group-partition oracle over-read (#9) was caught by:
a large-looking aggregate move that collapses to ~0 once 1–2 specimens are excluded. Checking it
before writing it up, as pre-registered, correctly prevents a second instance of that mistake.

**Prediction 1 confirmed.** The corrected sampler does not measurably change which perturbation
*structures* the D1 optimizer finds recoverable — reinforcing, from a second independent angle,
what the disentangling-probe null result (above) already showed: the sampler-realism fix and the
basin-map's structure-vs-magnitude mechanism (finding #3) are genuinely orthogonal problems, not
two views of the same one. Plot: `fig_A1_basin_map_leg_acc_old_vs_corrected.png`.
