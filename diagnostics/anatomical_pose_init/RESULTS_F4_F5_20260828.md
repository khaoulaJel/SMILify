# F4 and F5 — both FAIL their pre-registered bars. What each rules out.

Bars fixed in `REGISTER_F_series_20260828.md` (committed `a35e5539`) before either ran. Frozen C11
head, loaded strictly, held-out synthetic specimens, specimen-level splits.

---

## F4 — optimal-transport assignment: FAIL, narrowly, with a real effect

| seg | argmax | OT | relative | better | sign p |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `tr` | 0.0652 | 0.0624 | **4.3%** | 35/40 | 0.0000 |
| `fe` | 0.0808 | 0.0772 | **4.5%** | 33/40 | 0.0000 |

Bar was ≥5% relative on both segments with sign p < 0.05. **Both segments land just under it.**
The effect is real and extremely consistent — 35/40 and 33/40, p = 0.0000 on both — and it is
*not* large enough to clear the threshold set in advance. Recorded as FAIL. The bar had no PARTIAL
band; that was an omission in my pre-registration, and I am not inventing one after seeing 4.3%.

Mechanism checks pass, so the small gain is genuine rather than degenerate: assignment entropy
4.31 nats, and OT uses **1.19×** the template vertices argmax does — it spreads the assignment out
rather than collapsing onto a few vertices.

**What this rules out:** global assignment structure is worth ~4.5%, not the 30% the multi-hypothesis
oracle gap suggested was available. Combined with F2 (a trained ranker bought ~3%), two independent
attacks on the *selection* stage now both land in the low single digits. **Selection is not where
the headroom is.**

### A void arm, recorded rather than quietly fixed

The first F4 implementation used **balanced** OT with a uniform column marginal over all 10,235
template vertices — forcing 2,048 sampled points to spread across the whole template. The
registered void control (low-regularisation Sinkhorn must approach argmax) fired at 0.096
agreement. Inspection showed the control was right that something was wrong, but the fault was the
formulation: balanced OT's low-ε limit is the constrained OT solution, which has no reason to
resemble argmax. The arm was recorded VOID — it never validly tested the hypothesis, so its numbers
were not read as a result — and rebuilt as **unbalanced** OT, where the column marginal is a KL
penalty of strength τ rather than a hard constraint. τ→0 then provably recovers argmax, and the
control passes at **1.000**.

## F5 — conformal fit-quality intervals: FAIL, and the failure is the informative part

| | coverage | width |
|---|---|---|
| conformal interval from GT-free signals | 0.871 | 0.2320 |
| signal-free constant predictor | 0.869 | 0.2152 |

Coverage sits inside the registered [0.85, 0.95] band — but that is the trivial half of the bar,
since conformal coverage is guaranteed by construction. The width condition is the real test, and
it fails badly: **width ratio 1.078 against a bar of < 0.80.** The signal-based intervals are
*wider* than a constant predictor's. The GT-free signals carry essentially no information about
where the error is.

The mechanism check, registered precisely to catch this, is worse than the endpoint:

| segment | coverage |
|---|---|
| `ti` | 0.986 |
| `ta` | 0.929 |
| `tr` | 0.900 |
| `fe` | 0.886 |
| **`co`** | **0.657** |

**Worst-decile coverage: 0.200.** The intervals cover 87% of specimens overall and **20% of the
specimens that are actually worst** — precisely the ones a user needs flagged. Marginal coverage
holds while conditional coverage collapses exactly where the method's purpose lies. This is the
registered failure mode, and it is why coverage alone was never allowed to constitute a pass.

**What this refines, carefully:** `LAB_RECORD §8` established that cycle-consistency degrades
per-specimen on real scans and "doubles as a ground-truth-free triage signal". That claim was about
a **per-specimen binary** signal on real scans. F5 tested something different and harder — a
**per-segment quantitative** predictor of retrieval error on synthetic — and it does not survive
that. The §8 finding is not overturned; its scope is now bounded.

**What this means for the uncertainty gap:** SMILify still has no calibrated uncertainty, and the
cheap route to it is closed. Conformal machinery is not the problem — it worked exactly as
advertised and produced honest, useless intervals. The missing ingredient is a *signal that
predicts error*, and neither cycle-consistency nor embedding margin is one at segment level.
