# Q3a results: why CSE helps some real specimens and harms others

Exploratory case study on the 11 JAB specimens (PREREGISTRATION.md). Everything here was generated by
looking at the real test specimens, so every mechanism below is a **hypothesis**, tested only on
synthetic data in Q3b. All numbers come from scripts in this folder; outputs in `out/`.

## 1. Two different failures, at two different places in the pipeline

**A1, per stage** (`out/A1_stage_table.txt`): CSE beats production on 10/11 specimens at H0, 9/11 at
H1 and H2 (median −7.2% WL), 5/11 at S2 and 3/11 at S3 (median +2.4% WL). On real scans CSE helps
skeleton placement in the hierarchical stages, and the gain is lost in the surface stages, mostly in
the distal legs.

| failure | specimens | where it appears | what it looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| **wrong targets** | Cephalotes, Discothyrea, Eciton, Leptogenys | before fitting | 0.25-0.54 of leg targets on the right leg (proxy-corrected); many land off the legs entirely (non-leg 0.17-0.38) |
| **good targets, lost in the surface stages** | Aphaenogaster, Gigantiops, Cyphomyrmex, Odontomachus, Aenictus | after H2 | targets 0.73-1.00 leg-correct, CSE ahead at H2, behind or level at S3 |

Formica (production 44.8 → CSE 14.5% WL) is the clearest win: production puts only 26% of the leg
surface on the right leg, CSE 94%. Eciton is the other large win, even though its targets are poor:
production is worse still there (fit leg accuracy 0.17).

## 2. Mechanism measurements

**A2/A2b: what is wrong with the targets.** Proxy labels from the expert skeleton (validated on
synthetic: leg identity 0.892, segment 0.832; coxa excluded) show that CSE errors on real scans are
wrong leg **number**, almost never wrong **side**: side 0.00-0.05, number 0.10-0.37. Front, middle
and hind leg identity fails, not laterality. Corrected for proxy error, target quality is bimodal:
0.89-1.00 on four specimens, 0.25-0.54 on four.

**A2: correspondence and skeleton dissociate on real scans.** At S3, CSE fits put more leg surface on
the right leg than production on 10/11 specimens, including specimens whose joints CSE made worse
(Aphaenogaster 0.90 vs 0.87 with joints +11.9% WL). The surface lands on the right legs while the
pivots move away.

**A5: the fitter absorbs the correspondence term through every free channel.** Between H2 and S3,
under CSE: deformation RMS 2.4-8.7× production on 11/11; per-joint translation larger on 10/11; pose
change larger on 11/11; per-joint scales already inflated at H2. Pivots drift further on 7/11. The
exceptions are Formica and Gigantiops, the two specimens with the best targets.

**A4: the network is out of distribution on real scans.** Per-point leg accuracy is 0.86-0.99 on
synthetic and 0.23-0.80 on real. Cycle-distance medians are 0.013-0.023 synthetic and 0.022-0.260
real, with 10/11 above the synthetic range. Cycle filtering's within-specimen discrimination falls
from AUC 0.68-0.89 to 0.54-0.73, about 0.53 on Eciton and Leptogenys. As a specimen-level alarm it
flags Cephalotes and Discothyrea, and misses Leptogenys.

**A6/A7/A7b: the shift is articulation, not shape.** Real fitted shapes are *inside* the training
shape spread (betas Mahalanobis 2.4-9.6 vs training median 16.8). Real articulation is outside the
training pose spread, and this is measured without any fit: expert-skeleton leg bend relative to rest
is 18.1-38.2° (10/11 ≥ 25.9°) against a training per-specimen maximum of 19.0°. Annotation noise
cannot produce this: even 4× the measured repeatability raises the training maximum only to 28.7°.

**A8: real pinned specimens fold their legs in.** Distal leg joints sit 15.9-59.4% WL from non-leg
surface on 8/11 real specimens, against 74-137% WL on synthetic. The three real specimens inside the
synthetic range all have good targets.

## 4. Hypotheses carried to Q3b (synthetic tests)

| id | hypothesis | test |
|---|---|---|
| **H-artic** | real articulation (magnitude, and the folded posture of pinned specimens) is outside the training distribution, and that is what breaks the learned correspondence | S2: ART dose-response, ARTLIM, REALPOSE vs REALSHAPE vs REALSCALE vs REALALL |
| **H-absorb** | wrong targets are absorbed through scales, joint translations, deformation and pose, which moves the pivots | S1: coherent wrong-leg corruption at rho 0 / 0.2 / 0.4, `full` vs `hier` vs `prod` |
| **H-surfterm** | the surface-stage correspondence term hurts the skeleton even with correct targets (Aphaenogaster) | S1 rho = 0: `full` vs `hier` |
| (residual) | Cephalotes-type failure from anatomy the model cannot express | not reproducible synthetically; reported as unexplained if S2 leaves it |

The review's morphology-shift hypothesis is not supported as the *main* gap: real shapes sit inside
the training shape distribution.

## Limits

n = 11, hypothesis-generating only. The proxy is weaker at the coxa (excluded) and probably on real
scans than on synthetic (bounded in A2b). Fitted parameters (A5, A6) are themselves outputs of the
fits being explained; A7 and A8 are the fit-independent measurements and carry the articulation
conclusion.
