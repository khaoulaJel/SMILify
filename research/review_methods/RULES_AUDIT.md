# Rules audit: inherited rules checked against the state of the art

Every rule this programme carries over from earlier work, or that I introduced, is listed here with
its origin, the state-of-the-art check, and a verdict: KEEP, MODIFY or ADD. Changes to the frozen
protocol that follow from this audit are recorded in `DEVIATIONS.md`, dated, never edited into
`PROTOCOL.md`. Started 2026-10-06; extend it whenever a new rule is adopted.

| # | rule | origin | SOTA check | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Specimen is the unit of inference; median over joints, mean over seeds | JAB §8.1 | Hierarchical/nested data inflate false positives when pooled (Saravanan 2020). In human pose estimation the standard per-subject summary is the **mean** joint error (MPJPE). | **KEEP**, and **ADD** the mean alongside the median, so results are comparable with MPJPE-style reporting |
| R2 | Verdict = Hodges-Lehmann shift + exact signed-rank + Holm + TOST against a SESOI | JAB §8 | Demšar 2006 (signed-rank for paired methods over datasets) supports the test. Benavoli et al. 2017 argue NHST verdicts are poorly suited to few datasets and recommend the **Bayesian signed-rank test with a ROPE**, giving P(better), P(equivalent), P(worse). That fits a heterogeneous n = 11 better than a binary Holm verdict, which reads INCONCLUSIVE by construction (PROTOCOL §3.3). | **KEEP** as the pre-registered verdict; **ADD** the Bayesian signed-rank posterior (ROPE = SESOI) as a secondary, pre-declared summary |
| R3 | SESOI = 2.5% WL | JAB, borrowed from V11 surface-landmark repeatability (2.4% WL) | V11 is in the excluded V-series, so checked rather than trusted: the frame defect is `obj = (x, z, -y)`, a pure rotation (det +1), and V11 compares two rounds in the same frame normalised by WL from the same frame. **The 2.4% survives.** Literature: intra-observer error dominates landmark error on micro-CT surfaces (Robinson & Terhune 2017; ~1.5% relative TEM for inter-landmark distances). Remaining weakness: it is *surface* repeatability, and interior joint centres are probably less repeatable. | **KEEP**, with the limitation stated (also stated in JAB) |
| R4 | Holm family fixed at m = 6 | introduced 2026-10-06 | Standard FWER control; fixing m in advance prevents selective reporting. | **KEEP** |
| R5 | >= 2 seeds per synthetic fitter arm; interpret only above seed SD | Z5 measurement, Bouthillier 2021 | Bouthillier et al. 2021: seed variance changes benchmark conclusions; more seeds are better. JAB used 3. | **MODIFY**: 3 seeds on synthetic too, for consistency with JAB |
| R6 | Never select a method on chamfer | JAB arm J, M2 | Consistent with correspondence-benchmark practice: correspondence is scored against ground-truth maps, not surface distance (Kim et al. 2011 protocol). | **KEEP** |
| R7 | Retrieval error = Euclidean distance in canonical rest space / segment length | C3/C11/F-series | The shape-correspondence standard (Kim, Lipman & Funkhouser 2011, used by FAUST/SHREC) is **geodesic** error normalised by sqrt(surface area), reported as a **cumulative error curve**. Euclidean error under-counts cross-branch errors: two adjacent legs are close in space and far geodesically, exactly our dominant failure (wrong leg number, A2). | **MODIFY**: ADD geodesic error / sqrt(area) with cumulative curves as the primary correspondence metric (`diagnostics/correspondence_accuracy/geodesic.py` exists; verify it before use). Keep the old metric for continuity with C11/F-series |
| R8 | Cycle-consistency filter, keep 0.5, for CSE targets | C4 (beat cosine; cosine anti-correlated with correctness) | Standard filters: mutual nearest neighbour and Lowe's ratio test; current SOTA learns a per-match confidence (PDC-Net, Truong et al. 2021; Neural Localizer Fields σ). Our evidence: strict MNN failed, cycle worked. keep_frac 0.5 was never tuned on real data. | **KEEP** as the baseline filter; a learned confidence is a candidate only if Q2/Q3 point at uncertainty (M02/M06b) |
| R9 | Proxy-label gate: leg >= 0.85, leg segment >= 0.75 | introduced 2026-10-06 (Q3a) | No standard exists for a skeleton-derived proxy. The thresholds are a judgement: proxy error must be small next to the effects measured (leg-accuracy differences of 0.1-0.6). Weakness: observed accuracy is attenuated by proxy error. | **KEEP**, and **ADD** a proxy-error correction (invert the gate confusion matrix) as a sensitivity check before quoting real-scan correspondence numbers in the paper |
| R10 | Joint error normalised by Weber's length | JAB LITERATURE §3 | Standard size normaliser in ant morphometrics. | **KEEP** |
| R11 | FK rotation pivots as the primary model joint | JAB §5.1 | Alternatives pre-registered and shown not to change verdicts (JAB sensitivity). | **KEEP** |
| R12 | "Learned descriptors lose to rigid proximity" (W1/W2/W5/R11) | local evidence | Not a general law: learned correspondence beats ICP-style proximity widely in the literature (e.g. 3D-CODED, deep functional maps, LoopReg). Our result holds *within an already-correct part, at this data scale*. | **KEEP as local evidence only**; never cite it as a general claim |
| R13 | Pre-registered bar before each run | programme practice | Standard pre-registration practice. | **KEEP** |
| R14 | Synthetic body length L = GT bbox diagonal | introduced 2026-10-06 | No standard. Bbox diagonal is pose-dependent (splayed legs enlarge it), so it adds pose-dependent noise to a skeleton metric. | **MODIFY**: use a pose-invariant length, the sum of GT body-axis bone lengths (b_t -> b_a_* chain), computed from the GT FK joints. Record in DEVIATIONS before any synthetic skeleton number is produced |
| R15 | Q2 decision threshold: an oracle must close >= 40% of the B1 -> O-all gap to justify building the matching Q4 method | introduced 2026-10-06 (Q2 prereg) | No standard exists for this; information-budget analyses in the literature report fractions without a build/no-build cut. 40% is a judgement: large enough that a method recovering part of an oracle's value could still matter. | **KEEP as a pre-set decision heuristic only**; every gap fraction is reported with its CI so a reader can apply another threshold, and the paper presents the fractions, not the cut |
| R16 | Oracle re-optimisation = JAB oracle loop (Adam lr 0.005 on global_rot/joint_rot/betas/trans/deform) | JAB tools/oracle.py | Checked against production code: Stage_3 uses lr 0.0005 (10x lower), joint_rot lr 0.002, and `scheme: all` also optimises log_beta_scales and betas_trans. So JAB's loop is 'a' re-optimisation, not 'the production objective plus one term'. JAB's O1 numbers stand as JAB defined them. | **REPLACE** for Q2: subclass MoonshotStage with the exact Stage_3 options (DEVIATIONS D6) |
